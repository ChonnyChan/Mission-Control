How to get an iCloud calendar link
  1. iPhone: Calendar app → Calendars → tap ⓘ next to the calendar → turn on Public Calendar → Share Link… → Copy.
  2. Mac: Calendar → right-click the calendar → Share Calendar… → tick Public Calendar → copy the webcal:// link.
  3. Paste it below as-is — webcal:// links are fine.
“Public” means anyone with the link can view it; it isn’t listed or searchable anywhere.
How to get a Google Calendar link
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → ⚙ Settings.
  2. Under “Settings for my calendars”, click the calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.
Keep these links private — anyone with one can read that calendar.
